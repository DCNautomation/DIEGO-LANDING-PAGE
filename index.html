Automation projects

A sample of the n8n workflows I've designed and built end to end.

Facebook Chatbot with AI Agent

Webhook handles GET verification and POST messages; an AI Agent (Groq, with memory and tool access) pulls a knowledge base doc and replies via the Facebook Graph API.

Lead Router & Notifier

Reads leads from Google Sheets, branches them through conditional checks, sends Gmail notifications, and updates row status once processed.

Lead Enrichment & CRM Sync

Webhook captures new form submissions, enriches contact data via a third-party API, dedupes, and pushes clean records into HubSpot or Airtable.

Meeting Notes & Action Item Extractor

An AI Agent summarizes an uploaded meeting transcript, extracts action items into a project management tool, and emails a summary to attendees.

Job Application Tracker & Resume Checker

Parses incoming resumes on submission, scores candidates against role criteria, updates a tracking sheet, and auto-sends acknowledgment emails.

Invoice Processing Automation

Extracts key data from incoming invoices via OCR/AI parsing, validates against budget, and routes for Slack/email approval before logging to accounting.
